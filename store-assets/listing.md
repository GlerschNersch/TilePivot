**Full description (limit 4000):**

> TilePivot is a home-screen launcher built from big, flat tiles. Swipe sideways between
> channels, and each one opens with a large hero panel for its first item above a grid of
> smaller tiles.
>
> CHANNELS
> • home: your pinned apps first, then the apps you opened recently, then everything else
> • games, tv & movies and music: your installed apps, sorted by type automatically
> • apps: every app, A to Z, with search
> • your own folders, each as its own channel
> • settings: the launcher's options, plus shortcuts into Android's system settings
>
> MAKE IT YOURS
> • Five colour themes: Xbox Green, Midnight, Crimson, Violet and Carbon
> • Three tile sizes; pinch, or change it in settings
> • Gradient, solid or dimmed background, and a 12- or 24-hour clock
> • Hide the tv & movies or music channel if you don't use it
> • Pin apps to home, and group apps into folders
> • Back up your settings to a file and restore them later
>
> ACCESSIBLE
> TalkBack reads every tile and channel. Work-profile apps appear with their badge, and
> keyboard and gamepad navigation are supported.
>
> YOUR OWN 360 GAME LIBRARY (OPTIONAL)
> If you keep your own Xbox 360 game dumps on your phone (ISO, ZAR, Games on Demand or XBLA
> packages, or folders with a default.xex), pick their folder once in settings. TilePivot
> lists those games in the games channel, using cover images you place beside them, and
> opens them in a separately installed emulator such as Xendroid+. TilePivot only gets
> read-only access to the folder you pick, through Android's own folder picker.
>
> WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED
> TilePivot is a launcher only. It contains no games, no ROMs or game files, and no
> emulator. Xendroid+ is a separate app, not made by or bundled with TilePivot, and you
> obtain it yourself.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> TilePivot has no internet permission; its only permission lets you uninstall an app from its tile. No accounts, no ads,
> no analytics, no third-party SDKs; everything stays on your device.
>
> TilePivot is not affiliated with Microsoft or Xbox.

### Wording notes

- "Xbox" appears in the description only where the app's own UI uses it: the game format
  the library reads ("Xbox 360 game dumps"), the default theme's in-app name ("Xbox Green"),
  and the non-affiliation line. If the publisher renames the theme or the settings strings
  later, update this copy to match.
- No performance, battery or speed claims; no "free games" or download wording.

## Content rating notes (IARC questionnaire)

- App type: utility / launcher (not a game).
- No violence, sexual content, profanity, gambling, drugs or user-generated content in the
  app itself. It shows the user's own installed apps and, optionally, file names and images
  from a folder the user picks.
- No user-to-user communication, no sharing of location or personal info, no purchases.
- Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.
