TilePivot — Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

The short version: TilePivot collects no personal data. TilePivot does not have the Android internet permission. In fact it declares no Android permissions at all, so it cannot send anything off your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Everything TilePivot does happens on your device.

What TilePivot uses, and why

Your installed apps. TilePivot is a home-screen launcher. It reads the list of launchable apps on your device through Android's standard launcher APIs so it can show them as tiles. That list stays on your device.

Your own settings. Your pins, app folders, recently opened apps, theme, tile size and other preferences are stored only in TilePivot's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall TilePivot or clear its data. They leave TilePivot only if you use the in-app "back up settings" option, which saves them to a file in a location you choose. TilePivot does not upload that file anywhere.

A games folder, only if you pick one. If you choose a folder for the games library, TilePivot gets read-only access to that one folder, through Android's system folder picker. It reads file names and file headers there to list your games and show cover images. It never writes to the folder and cannot see anything outside it. You can remove the folder in TilePivot's settings, and you can revoke the access in Android's system settings at any time. The folder choice is not included in TilePivot's settings backup.

Launching a game. When you open a game, TilePivot hands its file path and title to the separate emulator app you installed (for example Xendroid+), on your device. That app is not part of TilePivot and has its own privacy practices.

Data sharing

TilePivot shares no data with anyone. It collects none, and it has no way to send any.

Children

TilePivot is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children.

Not affiliated

TilePivot is not affiliated with Microsoft or Xbox.
